About CampusEvents

A college event management site built for the Web Technology self-learning component, IT-25 (A & B).

Why this exists

Event notices on the department WhatsApp groups get buried within an hour. Registrations happen on three different Google Forms and nobody knows how many seats are left until the day itself.

CampusEvents puts the whole calendar on one page, tracks seats as people register, and gives organisers a single admin panel to manage events and export attendee lists.

What students can do

  • Browse upcoming and past events by category
  • Search by name, venue or organiser
  • See live seat availability
  • Register once and get a printable reference
  • Subscribe to the XML event feed
